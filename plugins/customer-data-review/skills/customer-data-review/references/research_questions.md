# Bootcamp research questions

Use these five unless the user supplies a different set. No automatic Core 10 or extended questions.

1. Where do customers describe problems they are solving with workarounds?
2. What outcomes do customers want that are not reflected in our current marketing?
3. What language do customers use about pain that is missing from our messaging?
4. Which customer problems span customer types and may represent an opportunity for us to own?
5. Where might there be a gap between what customers need and what we currently sell?

Questions 2, 3 and 5 require supplied messaging/product context for the comparison. Without it, report customer needs/language and explicitly leave the company-side comparison unverified. Customer calls cannot establish which internal team owns a problem; for question 4, internal ownership is a hypothesis to check with a colleague, never an inferred fact.

Assign stable IDs Q1–Q5 in request.json and plan.json. Keep unsupported questions visible as evidence gaps. The user may narrow or replace questions before the plan is locked; changes after extraction require a new scoped run, not silent cache reuse.
